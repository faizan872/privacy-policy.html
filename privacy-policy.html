Privacy Policy for KameetiX

Effective Date: November 1, 2026

Welcome to KameetiX ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you use our mobile application and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

1. Information We Collect

KameetiX is designed to help you manage your savings committees and rotation schedules locally and securely. We may collect minimal data necessary for app functionality, such as:

  • Committee member names and contribution records entered by the manager.
  • Local app preferences and settings.
  • Firebase authentication and Firestore database records associated with your active committees.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:

  • To manage and calculate committee turns, payouts, and contribution statuses.
  • To send local background reminders and notifications regarding your contributions and turns via WorkManager.
  • To display relevant advertisements via Google AdMob SDK.

3. Third-Party Services (Google AdMob & Firebase)

Our app integrates third-party services like Google AdMob for serving ads and Google Firebase for secure data synchronization. These services may collect information used to identify your device. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy regarding how they handle data.

4. Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at support@kameetix.com.
